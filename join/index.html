Federate with another person.

Two people, two machines, no server. One of you starts a connection and gets an invite blob; send it to the other over a channel you trust (Signal, in person). They paste it, send an answer blob back, and you connect peer to peer over real WebRTC. Then your two Didys run one signed query/answer exchange and settle a bounded provenance payout — each side verifying the other. This is the real thing; the /test proof shows it works cold and live.
